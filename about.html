About Carver Street Finds

Carver Street Finds is a personal guide to discovering interesting places around Manila. It features destinations where people can explore history, enjoy nature, discover local culture, and spend meaningful time with others.

The goal of this website is to make discovering places easier by sharing descriptions, recommendations, and helpful information about each destination.

Meet the Creator

I'm Rondale James Borondia, the creator of Carver Street Finds. I enjoy discovering different places around Manila, exploring new spots, and finding places where I can relax and spend time with friends.

Through this website, I want to share some of the places that make exploring Manila interesting and worthwhile. Whether it's a historic landmark, a peaceful green space, or a lively cultural district, every destination offers something different to discover.

What You Can Discover

History and Culture

Explore historic destinations such as Intramuros and discover places that reflect Manila's rich past.

Nature and Relaxation

Find peaceful spaces such as Arroceros Forest Park where visitors can enjoy greenery and take a break from the busy city.

Local Food and Experiences

Discover lively districts such as Binondo, known for its food, shops, and Chinese-Filipino culture.
