NATURE & OUTDOORS · MANILA

Arroceros Forest Park

Find a quieter side of Manila at Arroceros Forest Park, an urban green space where trees, plants, and shaded paths offer a refreshing contrast to the surrounding city streets.

Ideal for a peaceful walk, nature appreciation, and a short break from the busy city, the park also highlights the importance of protecting green spaces in a highly urbanized environment.

ABOUT THE DESTINATION

A Green Refuge in the City

Arroceros Forest Park is a recognized urban forest park in Manila. Its trees and vegetation provide an important green space within a densely developed part of the capital.

The park is valued for environmental education, recreation, and the experience of being surrounded by greenery in the middle of the city. It also represents the importance of maintaining urban nature for present and future generations.

Unlike Manila's historic landmarks and busy commercial districts, Arroceros offers a more nature-focused visit. Visitors can slow down, observe their surroundings, and appreciate the relationship between urban life and the natural environment.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Place Details

Location

Near the Manila City Hall area, Ermita, Manila, Philippines

Destination Type

Urban forest park, green space, and outdoor recreation area

Suggested Visit

Allow around 30–60 minutes for a relaxed walk and nature appreciation. You may need more time if joining an organized activity or educational visit.

Best Time to Visit

Morning is a good option for a more comfortable outdoor visit. Check the weather and avoid heavy rain.

Opening Hours

Park schedules can change. Confirm current opening hours with the relevant Manila parks authority before travelling.

Admission

Check current visitor policies and any applicable environmental user fees before your visit.

Getting There

The park is in central Manila near the Manila City Hall area. Plan your route using available public transportation, taxi, or ride-hailing services.

Recommended Activities

Nature walks, quiet breaks, plant observation, environmental learning, and nature photography.

What to Bring

Drinking water, comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, and a camera or phone.

Before you go: Opening schedules and visitor rules may change. Check announcements from the City Government of Manila before visiting.

RESPECT THE ENVIRONMENT

Visitor Tips

  • Check park opening hours before making the trip.
  • Bring water and prepare for Manila's warm weather.
  • Stay on permitted paths and follow posted rules.
  • Do not pick plants, damage trees, or disturb wildlife.
  • Keep the park clean and take rubbish with you when needed.
  • Keep noise at a considerate level so others can enjoy the space.
  • Follow instructions from park staff and respect restricted areas.
