About Intramuros

Intramuros is a historic walled area in Manila known for its Spanish-era architecture, old churches, museums, and preserved stone structures. It offers visitors a chance to experience an important part of Manila's history while exploring its streets and landmarks.

I recommend Intramuros because it is a great place to explore, take photos, learn about Philippine history, and spend time with friends. Its historic atmosphere makes it different from many other places around Manila.
