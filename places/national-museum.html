ART, HISTORY & CULTURE · MANILA

National Museum of the Philippines

Discover the Philippines through its art, historical collections, cultural treasures, and natural heritage. The National Museum offers a meaningful introduction to the country's identity and history.

From remarkable artworks to archaeological discoveries and natural history exhibits, visitors can explore the stories, traditions, and achievements that have shaped the nation.

ABOUT THE DESTINATION

Discover Filipino Heritage

The National Museum of the Philippines preserves and presents important collections connected to Philippine art, anthropology, archaeology, and natural history. Its museums help visitors understand the country's diverse cultural heritage and natural environment.

The National Museum complex includes different museums, each offering a distinct perspective on the Philippines. Visitors can explore historical objects, artistic works, and exhibits that tell stories about Filipino communities across different periods.

Whether you are a student, a history enthusiast, or a first-time visitor, the museum offers an opportunity to learn at your own pace and appreciate the stories behind the collections.

Things to See and Do

Discover artifacts and archaeological collections that provide insights into early communities and their ways of life.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Place Details

Location

Manila, Philippines

Destination Type

Museums, art, history, culture, archaeology, and natural heritage

Suggested Visit

Allow around 2–4 hours. You may need more time if you plan to visit multiple museums and explore their collections.

Best Time to Visit

Weekday mornings may offer a more relaxed experience. Check the museum's schedule before planning your trip.

Opening Hours

Schedules may vary by museum and special circumstances. Check the official website for current opening hours.

Admission

Check official visitor information for current admission policies, requirements, and any special exhibition arrangements.

Getting There

Public transportation, taxis, and ride-hailing services can take you to the museum district. Plan your route according to the specific museum you intend to visit.

Recommended Activities

Museum tours, art appreciation, historical learning, architecture viewing, and educational photography where permitted.

What to Bring

Comfortable shoes, drinking water, a small bag, and any student identification you may need. Follow the museum's rules on bags and personal belongings.

Before you go: Museum schedules, visitor policies, and exhibition arrangements may change. Check the official National Museum website before your visit.

TRAVEL SMART

Visitor Tips

  • Check current museum schedules before visiting.
  • Decide which collections you want to prioritize.
  • Allow enough time to appreciate the exhibits.
  • Follow posted rules on photography and personal belongings.
  • Do not touch artworks or objects unless explicitly permitted.
  • Keep your voice at a considerate level inside galleries.
  • Follow staff instructions and help protect the collections.
