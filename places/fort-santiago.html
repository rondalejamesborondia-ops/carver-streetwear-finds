HISTORY & HERITAGE · INTRAMUROS

Fort Santiago

Step into one of Manila's most recognized historic landmarks. Located inside Intramuros, Fort Santiago offers a closer look at the country's colonial past and its role in Philippine history.

Walk through its stone gateways, explore its historic grounds, and discover places connected to national hero José Rizal. The fort provides a meaningful experience for history enthusiasts, students, and travelers exploring old Manila.

ABOUT THE DESTINATION

A Fortress with a Story to Tell

Fort Santiago is a historic defensive fortress within Intramuros, Manila's walled city. Its history reflects different periods of Philippine life, including Spanish colonial rule and the country's struggle for freedom.

The fort is closely associated with José Rizal, who was imprisoned there before his execution in 1896. Sites connected to his final days help visitors understand an important chapter in the Philippine independence movement.

Today, Fort Santiago is a heritage destination where visitors can appreciate historic architecture, open courtyards, stone walls, and preserved spaces while learning about the people and events connected to the site.

TRAVEL SMART

  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking on uneven historic surfaces.

  • Allow time to explore other nearby Intramuros landmarks.
