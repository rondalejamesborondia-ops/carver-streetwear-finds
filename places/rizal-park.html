PARKS & NATIONAL HERITAGE · MANILA

Rizal Park

Discover one of Manila's most recognized public parks, a destination that combines open green spaces, monuments, and important stories from Philippine history.

Named after national hero José Rizal, the park offers space for sightseeing, quiet walks, photography, and learning about the country's struggle for freedom. It is a worthwhile stop for visitors exploring the heart of Manila.

ABOUT THE DESTINATION

A Green Space Rooted in History

Rizal Park, also known as Luneta, is a major historical and recreational landmark in Manila. It commemorates José Rizal, whose writings and ideas helped inspire the Philippine national movement during the Spanish colonial era.

The park is home to the Rizal Monument, a significant national memorial. Its grounds also include landscaped areas, pathways, gardens, and public spaces used for leisure, gatherings, and civic events.

Visitors can enjoy a relaxed walk while learning about the nation's history. The combination of greenery, monuments, and nearby cultural attractions makes Rizal Park a useful stop on a Manila itinerary.
