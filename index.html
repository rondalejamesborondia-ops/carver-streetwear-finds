I'm Rondale James Borondia, a student who enjoys exploring different places around Manila, discovering new spots, and finding places where I can relax and spend time with friends.

My Top Picks

  • Intramuros

    Historic place in Manila

    A historic area in Manila known for its colonial architecture and vibrant street culture.

    Why I Recommend This Place?

    I recommend Intramuros because it is a great place to explore, take photos, and spend time with friends while experiencing one of Manila's most historic areas.

  • Arroceros Forest Park

    A hidden gem in Manila

    A small urban forest in Manila that offers a peaceful escape from the city's hustle and bustle.

    Why I Recommend This Place?

    I recommend Arroceros Forest Park because it is a serene and green space in the middle of the city, perfect for relaxation and nature walks.

  • Binondo Manila

    The world's oldest Chinatown

    Binondo is the oldest Chinatown in the world, located in Manila, Philippines.

    Why I Recommend This Place?

    I recommend Binondo Manila because it is a vibrant and culturally rich area that offers a unique shopping and dining experience.
